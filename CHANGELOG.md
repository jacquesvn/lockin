# Changelog

Generated by `scripts/make-changelog.js` from two sources that cannot be quietly
edited to flatter a release: the `WHATSNEW` notes users were actually shown in the app,
and the git tag dates. Do not hand-edit — run the script.

Full downloads and installers: [Releases](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases).

## 0.54.0 — unreleased

- Lockin now notices when your CS2 config was written by an older version, and says so in Setup instead of quietly sending less than it could
- It asks CS2 for your loadout too, so a future card can tell you what you were holding when you died, not just what it cost

## [0.53.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.53.0) — 2026-08-13

- Fixed: leave Lockin open and it never noticed a new version — closing to the tray is not quitting, and the check only ran at launch

## [0.52.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.52.0) — 2026-08-13

- Showing up — the share of rounds you died in without a kill or an assist, tracked against your own earlier rounds
- It is the first figure on Progress that Lockin measured rather than asked you to type in

## [0.51.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.51.0) — 2026-08-12

- The half buy — when yours win less often than both your full buys and your full ecos, Lockin says so
- Casual and deathmatch rounds are left out of it, because nothing you buy there costs you anything

## [0.50.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.50.0) — 2026-08-12

- Your rounds, not the scoreboard — the round you got a 2k and lost is no longer filed with the round you did nothing
- Removed the panel that reported your median time of death. It was a fact with no decision attached

## [0.49.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.49.0) — 2026-08-12

- Your death audit now argues with the clock — tag deaths as aim when CS2 timed them in the opening seconds, and it says so
- It only ever claims a number it can prove, and stays quiet when the clock agrees with you

## [0.48.1](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.48.1) — 2026-08-12

- Fixed: the match debrief never appeared unless the plan called it a match night — it follows the match now
- Fixed: rounds you survived recorded zero kills, when CS2 was sending the real number all along

## [0.48.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.48.0) — 2026-08-12

- Fixed: CS2 auto-tracking recorded no rounds at all — the death audit had been blank since it shipped

## [0.47.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.47.0) — 2026-08-12

- Fixed: the stop-loss went quiet at midnight — it was silent for five of the six hours it exists for
- Fixed: “Do the ten” did nothing whenever a lapse or tilt card was on screen
- Fixed: coming back from a pause fast-forwarded the plan by the whole pause
- Fixed: past week 12 every week counted as a deload, so every drill stayed halved forever
- Fixed: “Erase all data” left every lineup screenshot behind
- Fixed: the reminder switch could read ON while your browser was blocking notifications
- Progress opens without the pause — your record is worked out once instead of on every render

## [0.46.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.46.0) — 2026-08-12

- CS2 now writes your death audit for you — Lockin watches each round instead of asking
- Fixed: after week one everyone was wrongly shown “a lighter week back” and lost their non-core drills
- Fixed: Resume Now was wiping the streak the pause card promised to hold
- The moment you finish your core drills lasts longer, and the violet is calmer
- On the web, Lockin now reminds you to export before a browser wipe can take your streak

## [0.45.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.45.0) — 2026-08-11

- The progress card can be copied straight to your clipboard, not just saved
- Twelve weeks at a glance, and milestones as a track you move along
- Your off-plan sessions are listed back to you instead of forgotten overnight

## [0.44.0](https://github.com/jacquesvn/lockin/releases/tag/v0.44.0) — 2026-08-11

- Gear reads your CS2 config — crosshair, sensitivity and resolution, each with a verdict
